Guitar Shops

High-end and vintage-leaning shops, from the Hyatt Regency in Polanco. There's no true vintage dealer in town, so think Custom Shop room plus a couple of shops worth digging through.

Worth the trip

Holocausto Music / Fender Custom Shop

Calle de Bolívar 76, Centro Histórico · ~25–30 min Uber

Fender Custom Shop roomCall ahead for showroom

Fender's first Custom Shop showroom in Latin America, on the building's rooftop. It opened as appointment-only, so call before going up. A recent visitor compared the Custom Shop selection to Sound of Music in Germany.

Calle Bolívar is the city's guitar street, so walk the block while you're there.

Tips

  • One stop only? Holocausto, for the Custom Shop room.
  • Pair them up: Rock Shop and Guitar Gear Roma are close together and easy to fit around the taco tour or dinner in Roma.
  • Sunday: only Holocausto is open.
  • Flying it home: keep the receipt, and plan to declare it at US customs on the way back.
